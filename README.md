# StratPool

**Expert strategies. Smarter trading. Shared success.**

## Introduction

Getting started in trading can be overwhelming. New traders often lack the time, experience, and tools to design and evaluate trading strategies, while experienced strategists have valuable expertise that isn't always accessible to others.

StratPool bridges that gap through a marketplace where professional traders and strategists can create and publish trading strategies, and aspiring traders can discover strategies suited to their goals and risk preferences.

Rather than asking users to rely on claims alone, StratPool evaluates strategies against historical market data from Uniswap, accessed through The Graph. Backtesting provides insight into how a strategy would have performed under past market conditions, helping traders compare options and make more informed decisions. A strategy-matching engine recommends relevant strategies based on a user's trading intent, and on-chain infrastructure supports their execution.

StratPool also makes strategy creation approachable with a visual, drag-and-drop workflow builder. Strategists can share their expertise without having to build every part of the trading infrastructure themselves.

## Our Vision

**We want to lower the barrier to trading without lowering the standard of trust.**

Our vision is to make trading expertise more accessible by connecting people who understand the market with people who want to participate in it. We believe aspiring traders should be able to explore clearly evaluated strategies, understand the risks involved, and choose what aligns with their goals—without needing to become experts before they begin.

At the same time, we want to give skilled strategists a meaningful way to earn from their work. StratPool's profit-based commission model is designed to align incentives: strategists earn commissions when trades using their strategies generate profits, rather than simply when someone subscribes or follows them.

By combining accessible strategy creation, historical evaluation, personalized discovery, and transparent on-chain execution, we aim to build a marketplace that rewards thoughtful strategies, encourages continuous improvement, and helps more people participate in decentralized finance with confidence.

*Historical backtesting is a tool for evaluating strategies, not a guarantee of future returns. Trading always involves risk.*
